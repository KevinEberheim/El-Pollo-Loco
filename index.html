How to Play

Run
Arrow keys left/right or "left"/"right" buttons

Jump
Spacebar/arrow up or "jump" button

Throw
Key D or "throw" button

Coins
Collect all coins to summon the Endboss

Normal Chicken
Jump on it or hit it with a bottle

Small Chicken
Can only be defeated by jumping on it

Endboss
Appears once all coins are collected; hit it with bottles to defeat it
